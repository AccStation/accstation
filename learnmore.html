Attention!

France has officially become the first country in Europe to ban social media for children under the age of 15. The ban will take effect on September 1, 2026, for new accounts, and on January 1, 2027, for existing users. Before granting access, platforms will be required to verify users' ages using a government-issued ID, FranceConnect, or facial recognition.
The United Kingdom has already introduced age verification requirements under the Online Safety Act. Age checks are now mandatory for access to online 18+ content, and platforms are expected to use robust methods to verify users' ages. The UK government is also preparing legislation that could restrict social media access for users under the age of 16, with age verification expected to play a central role. Meanwhile, Ofcom has stated that simple age-estimation methods based solely on user behavior are not sufficiently reliable.
